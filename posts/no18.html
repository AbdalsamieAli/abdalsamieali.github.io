  • A List of PSRC Standards and Guidance related to Power System Protection and Control


    2026-08-31

    The IEEE Power System Relaying and Control Committee (PSRC) is one of the 17 technical committees of the Power and Energy Society (PES) reporting to the PES Technical Council. it's the premier global authority on power system protection, operating under the IEEE Power & Energy Society (PES). For over 75 years, it has served as the central repository for industry standards and application guides related to protective relays .


    Standard CodeStandard NameDescription
    C37.90.1-2024Surge Withstand Capability (SWC) and Electrical Fast Transient (EFT)Specifies test procedures for immunity to surge voltages and fast transients in protection devices.
    C37.90.2-2024Radiated Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) ImmunityDefines radiated EMI test methods for relays and protection equipment in substation environments.
    C37.90.3-2023Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) TestsEstablishes ESD withstand capability requirements for protective relaying devices.
    C37.118.2-2024Synchrophasor Data TransferSpecifies the format and protocol for real-time transmission of synchrophasor measurements (wide-area monitoring).
    IEEE 1613Environmental Testing for Communication DevicesCovers environmental conditions (temperature, humidity, vibration) for relays with communication functions.
    C37.102AC Generator ProtectionProvides application guides for protecting synchronous generators, including stator, rotor, and abnormal frequency protection.
    C37.109Shunt Reactor ProtectionGuide for protection of shunt reactors against faults, overvoltages, and switching transients.
    C37.113-2025Transmission Line Relay ApplicationsOutlines best practices for selecting and setting relays for AC transmission line protection.
    C37.234Bus Protection GuideApplication guide for protective relaying of power system buses, including differential and backup schemes.
    C37.250System Integrity Protection Schemes (SIPS)Engineering and management guide for SIPS to maintain grid stability during extreme events.
