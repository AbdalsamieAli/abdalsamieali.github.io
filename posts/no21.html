  • The Reliability Of The Protection System


    2026-09-20

    The primary objective of the protection system is to maintain a high level of continue of service and when intolerable conditions occur to quickly disconnect and isolate only the faulty section , so the rest of the system continues in service with minimum damage.
    protection system monitor the power system for the president of a fault and initiate the appropriate control circuit to isolate the faulty suction, It is only minimize the duration of the fault it cannot anticipate the present of the fault .
    keeping this in mind they are five core facets of power system protection :

    1. Reliability: Assurance that the protection will perform correctly.
    2. Selectivity: Maximum continuity of service with minimum system disconnection.
    3. Speed of operation: Minimum fault duration and consequent equipment
    damage and system instability.
    4. Simplicity: Minimum protective equipment and associated circuitry to
    achieve the protection objectives.
    5. Economics: Maximum protection at minimal total cost.

    The last three ones speak for themselves, selectivity or discrimination is the ability of protection system to isolate only the faulty section (I will talk about it separately in another time).

    Reliability :

    According to the IEEE C37.120-2021 standard "The ability of a protection system to operate correctly taking into consideration both dependability and security ". Reliability has two aspects.

    Dependability: "The degree of certainty that a protection system will respond when the fault or abnormal condition the protection system is intended to detect is present". when interrable conditions occur the protection system should operate correctly and precisely. dependability is easier to a certain because we can test to protection system to ensure it will operate when a threshold value is exceeded.

    Security: "The degree of certainty that a protection system will not respond when the fault or abnormal condition the protection system is intended to detect is not present". It's Ensure that the protection system will not operate on terrible transient fault which is persist for very short time and clear itself, for this types of fault the protection system should not operate because it will case unnecessary outage and loss of service . As you may expect security is not easier because distinguishing between terrible and intellable fault without compromising dependability —the ability to trip when a fault actually occur—requires very precise settings and coordination.

    As a generality, enhancing security tends to decrease the dependability, and vice
    versa. security is very important as well as dependability and this conflicting requirement make protection system very interesting field this is why it's considered an art as well as technical science, meaning practice are far from standardized.
