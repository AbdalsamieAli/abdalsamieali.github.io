  • What tests must be performed on voltage transformers (VTs) before putting them into service?


    2026-10-12

    Regular maintenance and testing is very important in order to ensure that the protection system will perform as intended . This the difference tests that should be done to voltage or potential transformer before putting them into service?

    1. Insolation Resistance Test (IR test)
    Checks the insulation between the primary and secondary windings, and between windings and ground, using a high-voltage tester (Megger).

    2. Winding Resistance Test
    The CPC 100 injects a stable DC current through the winding and measures the resulting DC voltage drop across it. The resistance is then calculated internally.

    3. Polarity Test
    Confirms the primary and secondary windings have the correct relative polarity to ensure protection relays operate correctly.

    4. Ratio Test
    Applies a known voltage to the primary and measures the secondary output to verify the turns ratio.

    5. Burden Test
    Measures the actual connected load to ensure it remains within the VT's rated capacity, preventing accuracy errors.
