  • Electrical Circuit Analysis & Filter Design Using LTspice


    2026-11-5

    I’m pleased to share one of my practical projects: a collection of electrical circuit simulations and filter designs performed using LTspice.

    The project focuses on understanding circuit behavior, analyzing transient responses, and exploring the frequency response of different filter configurations.

    🔹 Circuit Analysis

    - First-order DC RC circuits.
    - Second-order DC series RLC circuits.
    - Second-order DC parallel RLC circuits.

    🔹 Passive Filter Design

    - RC Low-Pass Filter.
    - RC High-Pass Filter.
    - RLC Band-Pass Filter.

    🔹 Active Filter Design

    - First-order Active Low-Pass Filter.
    - First-order Active High-Pass Filter.
    - Active Band-Pass Filter.

    🎯 Project Objectives

    - Analyze voltage and current responses.
    - Understand transient and steady-state behavior.
    - Investigate filter frequency responses using Bode plots.
    - Verify theoretical calculations through simulation.
    - Strengthen practical skills in electrical circuit analysis.

    Through this project, I aimed to connect theoretical concepts from electrical circuit theory with practical simulation results.
