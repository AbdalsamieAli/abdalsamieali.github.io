  • Symmetrical Components Calculator


    2026-06-14

    Symmetrical components transform an unbalanced set of three-phase phasors into three balanced sequences:

    • Positive sequence
    • Negative sequence
    • Zero sequence

    This method significantly simplifies the analysis of unbalanced faults such as single line-to-ground (1LG), line-to-line (1LL), and double line-to-ground (2LG) faults.

    I developed a Symmetrical Components Calculator. The tool accepts a set of phasors, computes the positive, negative, and zero sequence components, and visualizes them on the complex plane.
